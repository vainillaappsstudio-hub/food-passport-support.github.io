# Food Passport — Support

Welcome to the Food Passport support page. Here you'll find answers to common questions and ways to get help.

## About Food Passport

Food Passport lets you mark and save dishes you've eaten and create food-related markers to build your personal food journey. Track your culinary adventures, discover new flavors, and keep a record of every meal worth remembering.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account to use Food Passport?

No. You can use all core features of Food Passport without creating an account. Your data is stored locally on your device.

### Where is my data stored?

Your saved dishes, food markers, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device. Depending on your device settings, some data may also be included in system backups provided by Google or Apple.

### Does the app show ads?

The free version of Food Passport may display ads through Google AdMob. The premium version is completely ad-free.

### How do I remove ads?

You can upgrade to the premium version of Food Passport through the in-app purchase option to enjoy an ad-free experience.

### Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete your saved content directly within the app. You can also uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data from your device.

### Is my data shared with third parties?

Food Passport does not sell your personal information. Some information may be shared with third-party services (such as advertising or map providers) solely to provide app functionality. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

## Troubleshooting

### The app is not working properly

Try the following steps:

1. Make sure you have the latest version of Food Passport installed.
2. Restart the app.
3. Restart your device.
4. If the issue persists, try clearing the app cache through your device settings.

### I lost my saved data

Data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstalled the app or cleared its data, local content may not be recoverable. If you had device backups enabled, you may be able to restore data through your device's backup and restore settings.
